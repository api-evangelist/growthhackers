––This article has been originaly published in the blog of Growthrocks.com by our Chief Strategist—Let’s be honest. Blogging isn’t dying. What’s dying is meaningless blogging. The kind of blogging that was created just to befriend Google’s algorithm.The kind of blogging stuffed with keywords, just to grab a backlink.The kind of blogging where the primary goal was to hit a “number of articles per month” quota to make the CMO or CEO happy.The kind of blogging that was thoughtless, soulless, and frankly, forgettable. That era? It’s over. And we all knew it was coming.
